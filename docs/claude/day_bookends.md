# Day Bookends, alarms and the evening — sub-file of the Schedule hub (`docs/claude/schedule.md`)
Modules `myDay` (wake, Good morning, Going to bed, evening review), `alarmSounds`, `tonightNotes`, `eveningQuestionnaire` (domain schedule). Day planning and blocks: `my_day.md`. Facts only; stories in `patch_notes.html`.

- Good morning (MD-5): `ui.openMorning` (+ `dashDeck.ui._fillBrief`), opened by ☀️ I'm up (`ui.imUp`) or the Morning Brief button — never by the clock. `_fillBrief` calls `morningBrief.ui._showSaved` so a reopen always shows today's saved brief (or writes it after 07:00 if none is saved). Today's realm picks and each Top 3 line carry ＋ Add to today (`ui.addTodayBtn` → `logic.placeNext`, next free 30 min, realm block); a Top 3 line's realm comes from its `[realm]` tag (`morningBrief.logic.top3Realm`, fallback Scholar for reviews/chapters, else Life).
- Evening review (MD-6; RP-7 Nov 5 2026: 🔮 Tonight's questions on top via `eveningQuestionnaire`, this summary folded under 📋 Today, note + Save in `#myday-evening-foot`; a not-done cooking block has ✓ Did it): `ui.openEvening(opts)` — from 🌙 Going to bed (`opts.bed`: Skip review / Save & next → `ui.openGoodnight`) or as yesterday's catch-up on I'm up (`opts.catchUp`); Tomorrow/Drop/Skip; `journal` row `evening_review` → +10 Mind. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-20-evening-review)
- Day Bookends: `myday_settings.day_state` {day, up_at, bed_at}; one button `ui.dayButton` on the dashboard (`#dd-ld-daybtn`, filled from `dashDeck._refreshGlance`) and My Day Now/Next (`#mdx-daybtn`). `logic.startDay` = wake_override to press time + alarm done + `logic.slideOrder` blocks moved later by the lateness via `moveBlockAfter` (Undo); `logic.reviewPending` offers yesterday's review if Going to bed was never pressed; `logic.endDay` + `logic.morningAlarm` (the alarm is pre-filled with tomorrow's Morning block start, earlier on a morning shift; `logic.suggestAlarm` = now + sleep target only when that time has passed). Awake = up within 20h, no bed press (`logic.awake`). `missedItems` counts Morning only on a day started with I'm up. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-27-day-bookends)
- Phone alarm (PA-1..5, Oct 28): `alarmSounds` (door: 🔔 in the 🌅 Morning block editor) lists `alarm_sounds` (public bucket `alarm_sounds`; link → `queued` → laptop `local_server.py` `import_alarm_sound` yt-dlp + ffmpeg 60 s mp3 → `ready`); edge fn `alarm-feed` (`q=status` RING/WAIT for due `wake`/`test:*` alarms with `phone_rang_at` null, picks a random sound into `myday_alarms.sound_url`, stamps `myday_settings.phone_last_seen`; `q=sound` 302; `q=stop`). MacroDroid steps: `alarmSounds.logic.SETUP`. Shares titled alarm/ringtone/wake up ask alarm-or-lesson (`shareInbox.ui.askAlarm`). [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-28-phone-alarms)
- Wake Flow (WF, Oct 29 2026): the in-app alarm is a full-screen cover (`ui._alarmCheck`, `#myday-alarm`) with one big ☀️ I'm up (`#myday-alarm-done` → `ui.imUp`: alarm row done, so the phone feed answers WAIT; then Good morning). The 20s tick also loads alarm rows (`logic.syncAlarms`, 10-min throttle), so a cold open shows the cover. Left menu ⚡ Actions (`#ln-actions`, built in `leftNav._buildDrawer`) opens `ui.openActions`: I'm up/Going to bed, Morning Brief, Doing now, I just did, Evening review. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-wake-flow)
- WF-5 (Oct 29 2026): `alarm-feed` (source now in `supabase/functions/alarm-feed/index.ts`) answers SOON when a wake/test alarm is due within 150 s; MacroDroid's second If (SOON → Screen On + Launch RPGACE) loads RPGACE early (a dark locked screen does not load it). `ui._alarmCheck` shows the cover from 150 s before the alarm (silent until due) and also for test rings (`logic.syncAlarms` loads `kind=wake` + `test:*`), where the button is ■ Stop test ring (marks only that row done, never starts the day). A `rpgace:login` hook runs the check straight after sign-in. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-wake-flow-2)
- Bedtime and wake asks (Oct 29 2026): `push_bookends()` (called by `push_digest()` after `push_alarm`, run each minute by pg_cron `myday-push`) sends "🌙 Going to bed now?" once at 23:00 unless Going to bed was pressed in the last 12 h, and "☀️ Are you up?" once at 07:00 only when today has no `myday_alarms` wake row and the day wasn't started; both `sticky` (sw.js `requireInteraction`). Taps open `?open=bed` → `ui.goingToBed`, `?open=up` → `ui.imUp`. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-bed-up-asks) Until answered, the ask is re-sent every 5 min (23:00–23:59, 07:00–12:59) with `repeat: true`; `sw.js` closes the old one and posts it new and silent so it sits at the top ([story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-asks-stay-on-top)).
- Good night closes the app (Oct 29 2026): after `logic.endDay` saves (review already in journal), `ui._closeForNight` tries `window.close()` and otherwise reloads to `?slept=1`, so the morning always runs fresh code. `RPGACE._noNightClose` turns it off (tests). [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-bed-up-asks)
- Push priority (Oct 29 2026): `myday-push` sends with `urgency: high` (source in `supabase/functions/myday-push/index.ts`, deployed v2); sw.js shows every RPGACE alert with renotify/vibrate, never silent. Ranking against other apps is the phone's per-app setting.
- Night tasks (Oct 29 2026): routine "💧 Fill a cup of water for your bedside" (daily, evening). Night tasks show only in the 🌙 Night card (`logic.eveningModel` leaves them out of Not done); on 🌙 Going to bed, Save & next refuses until they're ticked. Good night is two steps: 📋 Plan tomorrow (or Skip) → then the alarm, and the app closes only after the alarm choice. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-10-29-bed-up-asks)
- WR-10: `logic.awake` = up and no Going to bed, no 20-hour cut-off; `logic.staleDay` (20 h+) → `ui.askStaleDay` ("Still <day>? 🌙 going to bed / ☀️ new day", once per 3 h, on sign-in and when the app comes back); `logic.endDay(alarmAt)` also writes `wake_override` so the Morning block starts at the alarm time.
- Bedtime flow + weekly review: in `openEvening` (flow = bed/catchUp), Save & next and Skip open `ui.openReview(rv, next)` when `logic.reviewTarget(now)` is set and `reviewDone(rv)` is false; `openReview(ws, onDone)` calls onDone from the popup's onClose, so the alarm step follows. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-11-03-sunday-review)

- Bedtime owns the wake alarm: `logic.endDay` first sets every waiting future `myday_alarms` wake row to off, then inserts tonight's (if chosen). [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-11-04-no-alarm)
- Notes for tonight (RP-5, Nov 5 2026, module `tonightNotes`, domain schedule): one journal row per note (`source 'tonight_note'`, `entry_type 'note'`, `review_on` today, `realms` = `realmPages.logic.contextRealm`). Ways in: 📝 on every area bar (`navigation.bar`, `.area-note-btn`, `opts.noNote` hides it), 📝 Note in Live now (`#rt-live-note`, realm = Live now's), `note:` / `note for tonight:` in Oracle chat (`window.sendChat` wrap, `_tonightNotesPatched`). A phone share asks Note or Take in (`shareInbox.ui.askShare`): Note keeps the link in the note, no realms; Take in picks realms then asks why (`ui.askWhy`) → `intel_jobs.why`. [story](../../patch_notes.html#card-2026-11-05-rp5)
- Evening questionnaire (RP-7 + FF-4, Nov 5 2026, module `eveningQuestionnaire`, domain schedule): mounted at the top of `myDay.ui.openEvening` (`#eq-box`); the old review sits folded under `#myday-evening-today` ("📋 Today"), its note + Save stay visible in `#myday-evening-foot`. `logic.facts(day)` gathers not-done, nothing-logged, tomorrow's blocks, red trackers, new-task counts, tonight notes, today's take-ins and the Steward target; ONE call (`RPGACE.prompt('evening_questions')`, `claude-sonnet-4-6`, ≤1000 tokens) writes questions; `sanitize` + `rank` keep ≤5 per realm by urgency × importance, order day → Producer → menu order. One at a time (default) or See all (folded rows per realm). Yes with a plan shows it, then `makePlan` puts a block tomorrow (`myDay.logic.placeNext`) or keeps a task (`realmTasks.logic.keepIdea`). Answers: one journal row `source 'evening_questions'` per day (reopening reuses it, no second call). FF-4: open `feature_findings` rows (private, `kit.readPrivate`) join as 🧪 questions with no call; Yes/No + why go back on the row (`status`, `answer_why`).
