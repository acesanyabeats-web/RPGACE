RPGACE — what it is, in plain language

🐂The Minotaur Map

RPGACE is a personal AI operating system for making music and content — one place that plans, remembers, and helps carry an idea from a first thought all the way to something real and finished. The Minotaur is a piece of information — a question, a book, a beat, a recipe — carried through the app. This page shows the real shape of what RPGACE actually does, domain by domain, and what AI does the work — without the code-level detail; that lives in a separate, technical set of pages.

The Core
💬 Oracle — the assistant everything else talks to
One AI assistant, reachable from anywhere in RPGACE — the same assistant that generates a recipe also writes a treatment for a music video or answers a question about the app itself
Oracle is a chat assistant, but it's grounded — before it answers, it's given the real, current facts about RPGACE and about you, so it doesn't confidently make things up about its own app. It's reachable from the main chat, from a floating button on any page, and from inside specific tools (a recipe card, a beat's production panel) where it already knows the context of what you're looking at.
Grounded answers
told the real, live state of the app first, so "what's built" questions get honest answers, not guesses
Context-aware
open on a recipe or a beat's production panel and it already knows what you're looking at, without re-explaining
Catches good ideas
flags a reply worth keeping and offers to save it properly, rather than letting it scroll away
Confirm before it acts
anything Oracle can actually DO (not just say) shows you exactly what it would do first — you approve or decline
Learning
🌳 The Knowledge Tree
A single, growing structured library — every real idea worth keeping, wherever it came from, ends up organized into one place
Ideas get sorted into real subject areas — things like music theory, filmmaking, business, cooking, education, and more — each broken down into its own real structure, not just a flat pile of notes. New ideas are checked against the whole tree first, so the same concept doesn't get filed twice under two different names; two genuinely related ideas from different subjects can also get linked to each other.
Real subject areas
a genuine library spanning many disciplines, not just music — growing as more gets fed in
You get the final say
every new idea is shown exactly where it would go before it's made permanent — nothing is filed without your approval
Duplicate-aware
a genuinely new idea gets a new entry; the same idea again extends what's already there instead of cluttering it
Cross-subject links
a real connection between two distant ideas (different subjects entirely) can be surfaced and confirmed
Write it up
turn any branch of the tree into a real, readable article on demand
Reading
📖 Bookworm
Feed it a whole book — a link, a typed contents page, or your own file — and it reads it chapter by chapter, offering back what's genuinely worth keeping
A book you upload is read only on your own device before anything else happens — it's never sent anywhere first. Bookworm finds the real chapter list, lets you confirm it's right, then works through the book one chapter at a time, surfacing real ideas for you to approve, skip, or fix as it goes. Finished books land on a real, searchable shelf.
Three ways in
a link, a hand-typed contents page, or your own purchased/downloaded file
Chapter-by-chapter
real ideas surfaced as you read, not dumped all at once at the end
Feeds the Knowledge Tree
everything you approve becomes a real, permanent part of what RPGACE knows
A real bookshelf
every book you've read stays browsable, with what it taught you attached to it
Creative Work
🎬 Content & Video Pipeline
A beat or a video idea becomes a real, trackable project — carried through real stages from first idea to something posted
Logging a beat starts a real, numbered project you can always return to, with Oracle staying available alongside it the whole way through. A tutorial-style video moves through planning, filming, and editing; a beat-to-music-video project gets its own real stages — matching reference tracks and visual style, writing a script and visual treatment, editing that script, and finally packaging everything to hand off to a real video-generation tool (RPGACE plans and briefs, it doesn't render video itself).
Track a project start to finish
a real entry that doesn't fade once you scroll past it, with stages you can move through and revisit
Reference matching
finds tracks/artists with a similar real feel to a logged beat (tempo, key, mood, genre)
Director-style blending
blend the visual style of up to 3 real film directors into a written treatment for a video idea
Captions & listings
generates real caption/description text for Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok, and a Beatstars listing draft for a beat you're selling
Hand off to render
packages the finished brief for an outside video-generation tool — RPGACE briefs and tracks, it doesn't generate the video itself
Daily Life
🍳 Cooking
Recipes, planned cook sessions, pantry tracking, and a real shopping list — generated and kept in sync with what you actually have
Describe what you feel like eating and Oracle generates a real recipe, scaled to your servings, with a step-by-step timeline and timers. Save it, cook it now, or schedule it into a real multi-recipe cook session that plans around your own time and equipment. Your pantry, fridge, freezer, and shopping list all stay connected — a recipe card shows, at a glance, what you already have, what's running low, and what you'd need to substitute or buy.

Schedule a cook

Know what you have

A real grocery list

Nutrition, honestly
real nutrition data per recipe, pulled from genuine food-composition sources, not guessed

Under The Hood, Briefly
🧠 The AI Itself
What's actually doing the thinking, and why it's not the same AI for every job
RPGACE is built on Claude, made by Anthropic — the same AI family behind Oracle's own answers, recipe generation, taxonomy placement, video treatments, and everything else that needs real judgment. Not every task needs the same amount of "thinking" though, so RPGACE deliberately uses more than one tier, matched to the job:
Judgment work
deciding where an idea belongs, writing something you'll actually read, scoring how good a fit really is — the most capable tier handles this, since getting it wrong is the expensive mistake
Research & outlining
a lighter tier for scanning/summarizing large amounts of text before the judgment tier ever sees it — cheaper, and it's a job that doesn't need the heaviest model
Mechanical cleanup
the cheapest tier, reserved for pure formatting/rewording with zero real judgment involved — never used for anything that actually decides something
What this page is, and isn't. This is a real, honest picture of what RPGACE actually does — every domain and function named here is really built, not aspirational. What it deliberately leaves out is the internal engineering: no code, no database structure, no internal file names. There's a separate, much more technical set of pages for that (the development side of the project). If a genuinely new domain or capability ships, this page gets updated to include it; small internal changes underneath an existing domain don't need a new page here.
