RPGACE — how it thinks

🐂The Minotaur Map

A plain-language picture of how information moves through RPGACE — where something goes in, what happens to it, and where it comes back out. The Minotaur is a piece of information — a question, a book, an idea worth keeping — carried like water down a river. This page shows the journey, not the machinery. There's no code here, no table names, no internal wiring — just the real shape of what happens when you use RPGACE.

How to read this
🐂 The Minotaur — whatever you actually gave RPGACE
🌊 A river — one real journey that information can take
🌀 An estuary — where separate rivers meet and become one shared record
🏖️ The sea — where it finally lands somewhere you can see and use it
River One
💬 Talking to Oracle
The most common journey — from a question to a real answer
You ask something
in chat, or from any page's own quick-ask button
Oracle answers
grounded in what RPGACE actually knows about itself and about you — it's told the truth first, so it can't confidently make something up
If the reply is worth remembering
a small prompt offers to save it properly, rather than letting a good idea just scroll away and disappear
🏖️ You see the answer, and — if you say yes — it's kept
🌊 anything worth keeping flows into → River Three, Deciding Where an Idea Belongs
River Two
📖 Reading Books & Research
The longest journey — a whole book, or a piece of research, carried from cover to a finished, searchable shelf
🌊 A link, a hand-typed contents page, or your own file
The chapters are found first
a file you upload is only ever read on your own device — it isn't sent anywhere first
You're shown the chapter list, to confirm it's right, before anything else happens
You read a chapter
real ideas in it are found, and offered back to you one at a time
For each one — you approve it, skip it, or fix it yourself
🏖️ The book itself, and everything worth keeping from it, both end up on your shelf
🌊 every kept idea flows into → River Three, Deciding Where an Idea Belongs
River Three
🌳 Deciding Where an Idea Belongs
Every idea, wherever it came from, goes through the same real check before it becomes permanent
An idea worth keeping — from a chat, a book, or typed by hand
A real fit is worked out
where it belongs, how confident that placement actually is, and whether it's genuinely new or already covered elsewhere
You get the final say
shown exactly where it would go before it's real — nothing is ever written permanently without you seeing it first
🏖️ Accepted ideas become a real, permanent part of what RPGACE knows
🌊 flows into → River Five, One Shared History
River Four
🎬 Tracking Creative Work
A beat or an idea becomes a real, trackable piece of work, carried through its own real stages start to finish
🌊 A beat gets logged, or an idea gets saved as a project
It becomes a real, numbered piece of work
not a chat message that fades once you scroll past it — a real entry you can always come back to
It moves through its own real stages
planning, then making, then finishing — Oracle stays available alongside it the whole way, so the conversation and the tracker never drift apart
🏖️ Marked finished, with the real details of what shipped
🌊 flows into → River Five, One Shared History
River Five
📜 One Shared History
Everything above eventually lands in one place — a real, honest timeline of everything that's actually happened
Ideas kept, books finished, work shipped, money logged
🌊 The Confluence
everything merged into one current, sorted by when it actually happened — not scattered across a dozen separate places
A glance
the most recent few things, always visible on the dashboard
A deliberate look
the full history — searchable, and honest about where each thing came from
🏖️ Tap anything — see the real story behind it, not just its name
🌊 this is the real end of the journey for everything else on this map
River Six
🪞 Staying Honest About Itself
RPGACE keeps its own notes on what's actually built, what's still being worked on, and what's only an idea — so nothing pretends to be finished before it really is
If you ask what's built or what's still open
Oracle answers from RPGACE's own real, current notes about itself — not a guess, and never a claim that something works before it's actually been checked
🏖️ A straight answer — including "not built yet" when that's the truth
What this page is, and isn't. This is the plain-language shape of RPGACE — the real journeys information takes, without the internal detail of how any of it is actually built. There's a separate, much more technical set of pages for that (the development side of the project) — this page exists so the picture stays readable without needing any of that. If how RPGACE actually works ever changes in a way that changes one of these journeys, this page gets updated to match; day-to-day changes underneath a journey don't need a new page, only a genuinely new journey does.
