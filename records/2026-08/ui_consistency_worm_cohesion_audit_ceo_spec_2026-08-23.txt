── COMPLAINT 1: "no choice between bookworm and other worms - bookworm just
   appears while other hide behind buttons" ──────────────────────────────

CONFIRMED, root cause found by direct read of `dashDeck._openBookworm()`
(rpgace_core.js, currently line 10773):

  1. The popup is built with Bookworm's own identity (eyebrow "📖 Bookworm",
     accent var(--purple), title "Work through books, chapter by chapter").
  2. The pre-existing `#bookworm-widget` (Bookworm's real book-list — the
     actual card-per-book UI) is appended into the popup box FIRST, before
     anything else — it renders immediately, full-width, at the top.
  3. ONLY AFTER that does the "Choose a worm:" heading + 3 worm-picker
     buttons (Videoworm / Videoworm-Find-Videos / MusicWorm) get appended,
     BELOW the book list, needing a scroll past however many books exist.
  4. There is no "Bookworm" entry in the picker list itself — Bookworm isn't
     offered as a peer choice, because you're already standing inside it.
     The other 3 items are secondary jump-buttons bolted onto a page whose
     real identity and default rendering already belongs to one specific
     worm (Bookworm), not to the family as a whole.

This exactly matches Alex's framing — Bookworm functions as the container
and default view; the other worms are demoted to buttons inside someone
else's house rather than being presented as 3-4 equal peer choices.

── COMPLAINT 2: "research tab is not fully removed in those pages as
   references" ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONFIRMED, several real, distinct instances (not one bug, a genuine pattern):

  a) `index.html:244` — `<div class="section-title">📚 Research Lab ✦</div>`
     is the literal, still-live section title for `#page-learning`. Every
     real content panel under it (Videoworm/Video-Finder/Upload-Workshop)
     has already been rebranded and relocated elsewhere (Bookworm/Content
     Pipeline popups), but the page's own header text was never updated to
     match — it still announces a feature that's been fully retired.

  b) `#page-learning` is confirmed UNREACHABLE via any real navigation:
     `leftNav._items()` dropped its "Research Lab" entry Aug 15 2026 (own
     comment: "entry retired... Bookworm has no leftNav entry of its own
     yet"), and no dashDeck card points at it either. Yet the page is still
     load-bearing: 5+ separate injector functions
     (`beatLog._inject`, `refCorpus._inject`, `conidPot._injectIdeaBank`,
     `bookworm._injectBibliographySection`, `taxTreeManual._injectManualButton`)
     all resolve their target container via
     `document.getElementById('page-research') || document.getElementById('page-learning') || document.querySelector('[id*="research"]') || document.querySelector('[id*="learning"]')`
     — meaning the orphaned, stale-labeled "Research Lab" page is still the
     real DOM staging area these widgets get built into before dashDeck's
     popups relocate them (the established "MOVE, never rebuild" pattern).
     Functionally this still works — but the page itself, with its stale
     title, remains structurally necessary rather than genuinely retired.

  c) `document.getElementById('page-research')` — the FIRST clause in every
     one of those 5 fallback chains — resolves to nothing: no element with
     id `page-research` exists anywhere in `index.html`. It's a confirmed-
     dead reference to an even older page id, predating the current
     `page-learning` id, never cleaned up. Harmless (the `||` chain always
     falls through), but a textbook "reference not fully removed."

  d) `dashDeck._openResearch()` (rpgace_core.js:10882) is a second, wholly
     separate function still carrying `eyebrow: '🧠 Research Lab'` — real,
     but confirmed genuinely dead code (its own July-23-era comment,
     restated Aug 15: "kept defined but unreferenced... in case a future
     pass wants it back"). No live path calls it. Lower priority than (a)-
     (c) since nothing user-visible can ever trigger it, but worth a
     decision (delete vs. keep-as-is) rather than leaving it unexamined.

  e) A related, DATA-correctness instance beyond page text: the renamed
     "Upload Workshop" (was "Video Workshop", renamed Aug 15 2026) still
     literally saves new Encyclopedia entries titled `'Video Workshop — ' +
     date` (rpgace_core.js:3930/3939/3945) and its own empty-state copy
     ("...or use the Video Workshop.") still says the old name in BOTH
     `index.html:546` and a duplicate string in `rpgace_core.js:3432`. This
     is more than cosmetic — every real Upload Workshop save from Aug 15
     onward is being written into Encyclopedia under the WRONG, retired
     name, permanently, going forward, not just a one-time historical
     record.

── COMPLAINT 3: "music corpus was not fully absorbed" ─────────────────────

CONFIRMED, by direct comparison of `_openBookworm()` vs `_openCorpus()`
(rpgace_core.js:11124) vs the Videoworm panel's own real rebrand:

  - Corpus opens as its own COMPLETELY SEPARATE popup: different eyebrow
    ("🎼 Corpus" vs Bookworm's "📖 Bookworm"), different accent color
    (var(--blue) vs var(--purple)), different title copy, its own footer
    note. Nothing in the popup itself carries the "MusicWorm" identity —
    the jump-button that opens it says "🎼 Open MusicWorm (Corpus)", but
    the destination never says "MusicWorm" again.
  - Compare: Videoworm's rebrand DID carry all the way through — the real
    panel itself has `<h3>🐛 1 — VIDEOWORM</h3>` as its own heading. Corpus's
    equivalent heading (inside `refCorpus._inject()`) still reads "Reference
    Corpus" internally, never "MusicWorm."
  - Net effect: MusicWorm is a label that exists for exactly one click (the
    jump-button), then vanishes — the actual destination reads as an
    unrelated, pre-existing feature you were redirected to, not a "worm"
    inside the same family as Bookworm/Videoworm. This is a precise,
    concrete version of "not fully absorbed": the rename/absorption is
    skin-deep at the picker level and doesn't extend into the real page.

  Real, honest counter-point (see Part E) — Corpus's own jump-button note
  ALREADY says plainly "Reference-track library. Insight extraction not yet
  built." — i.e. this is a case where the visual distinctness may be
  partially ACCURATE, not just inconsistent: MusicWorm genuinely doesn't
  have the same taxonomy-insight-extraction pipeline Bookworm/Videoworm do
  yet (W3 of the Worm Family plan flagged this explicitly). A full visual
  merge that implied parity would arguably be MORE misleading, not less —
  worth resolving as an honest design decision, not a blind "just restyle
  it to match" fix.
