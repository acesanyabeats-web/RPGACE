**Fork 1 — rendering mechanism.** The reference image is a hand-laid-
out, curated illustration (~80 skills, positioned by a human artist
for legibility and visual balance). RPGACE Total Systems, at the
"break down until each node has its own logical path" depth Alex
asked for, is a different order of magnitude — 955 river-tagged nodes
in `graph.json` alone, before counting main.js's ~240 keyword-matched
nodes, before counting the external galaxies. A single flat image at
that node count cannot read the way the reference image reads, no
matter the styling — it would be an unreadable wall of nodes.
Two real, honest options:
  (a) **Drill-down, matching "as many times as it takes" literally.**
      Top level shows only the galaxies (Orchestrator CC/OpenMontage
      CC/Graphify CC/external providers/RPGACE architecture) as a
      small number of large stylized nodes, styled close to the
      reference image. Click a galaxy → a fresh radial view scoped to
      JUST that galaxy's own children (e.g. RPGACE architecture →
      its 16 rivers, styled the same way). Click a river → its real
      functions/buttons. Each level is its own small, legible,
      reference-image-styled diagram; the FULL depth Alex asked for
      exists, just spread across levels instead of one flat image.
  (b) **One giant flat image, real depth, accept illegibility at the
      full node count**, and cap the literal per-node breakdown at a
      shallower real tier (e.g. river-level, not function-level) to
      keep it as one screen. This does not honor "break down... until
      each node has its own logical path" literally.
  Recommendation: (a) — it's the only option that actually delivers
  what he described ("as many times as it takes"), and it reuses the
  EXACT same real river/module data already gathered rather than
  inventing a new shallower taxonomy.
