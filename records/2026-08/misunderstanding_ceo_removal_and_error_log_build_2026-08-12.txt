PART 2 — THE NEW error_log.html SYSTEM — real, honest scope
================================================================

Full spec compiled faithfully from Alex's own words (not paraphrased away
from what he actually asked): every real error any AI/code in RPGACE
Total systems reports → deduplicated by signature (repeat occurrences
increment a counter + track timestamps, never mint duplicate rows) →
rationalized (real diagnosis) → surfaced in `smoke_test.html` as a
separate "Just Failed" top group → a fix gets planned (through the normal
dev-process discipline, explicitly NOT branded `/CEO` per Part 1's own
correction) → `future_integrations.html` tracks it red→yellow→green →
on green, `smoke_test.html` highlights the change (yellow bg, black
border, 💡 bottom-left) marked "not confirmed" → Alex hand-tests → on
confirm, the linked error is marked resolved (not deleted — the past
error log is explicitly "vital for future development") → a short patch_
notes.html card + a real, generalized CLAUDE.md rule-extraction pass when
the error class is likely to recur elsewhere → if the fix DIDN'T work,
attempt count increments, a real diagnosis of why it failed is recorded,
and the loop repeats until confirmed.

**Real, mid-spec design correction Alex made to himself, honored
directly**: the full error timeline + lessons-learned should live on the
NEW doc, not as a section inside `patch_notes.html` — "to de-clunk and
narrow the specialisation of patch notes." `patch_notes.html`'s own role
bullet updated to state this split explicitly.

**Real, additive Supabase schema built** (safe — `CREATE TABLE`/`ADD
COLUMN` only, matches the CEO-framework additive-migration precedent):
- `error_log` — `error_signature`/`error_type`/`source`/`message`/
  `status` (active/planned/building/built_unconfirmed/resolved)/
  `occurrence_count`/`first_seen_at`/`last_seen_at`/`rationale`/
  `backtrack_note`/`attempt_count`/`attempts` (jsonb array)/
  `linked_plan_item_id` (FK to `ceo_plan_items` — an internal tracking
  link only, never rendered with CEO branding anywhere)/`resolved_at`/
  `lessons_learned`. `anon_all`/`authenticated_all` RLS, matching
  `session_memory`/`graphify_jobs`'s established posture.
- `smoke_test_items` gained 2 columns: `needs_confirm_highlight`
  (boolean) and `linked_error_id` (FK to `error_log`).

**Real, built, verified pieces (not just schema)**:
- `RPGACE.register('errorLog', {...})` in `rpgace_core.js` — a real,
  minimal client-side `window.onerror`/`unhandledrejection` capture,

  fix now also PATCHes the linked `error_log` row to `resolved` in the
  same action — one click, two real state changes, matching Alex's own

- `error_log.html` — new, real, working (d)-layer doc: Active / Being
  Fixed / Resolved groups, each real error's rationale/backtrack/attempt-
