H13: GROCERY LIST AUTO-SORT BY AISLE — Sep 16 2026
==========================================================================

ALEX'S VERBATIM ASK
------------------------------------------------------------
"Make grocery list auto sort by aisle, too much scrolling and calculating
needed"

REAL DESIGN CHOICE (checked before building, not assumed)
------------------------------------------------------------
Considered a new `ingredients.aisle` Supabase column (schema migration +
per-ingredient categorization), but:
- A migration is always Tier-3 territory (CLAUDE.md's own Judgment
  Funnel) and needs a real stop-and-confirm outside a ratified /CEO plan
  or /auto — extra friction for a genuinely small UI ask.
- A DB column still needs a real backfill for the 2 already-saved
  recipes' ingredients, and wouldn't classify a BRAND NEW ingredient the
  moment it's typed into a fresh recipe without a second write.

Built instead as a pure client-side, zero-AI-cost, zero-schema-change
keyword classifier — same "mechanical tier" discipline as cofid_foods'
own base_ingredient/prep_state grouping columns (rule 12's guide). No
migration, no backfill, works on any ingredient name (existing or brand
new) the instant the list renders.

THE REAL FIX
------------------------------------------------------------
`cookingOracle.logic._AISLE_GROUPS` — 9 real UK-supermarket-shaped
buckets (Fruit & Veg / Meat & Fish / Bakery / Pantry & Tins / Spices,
Sauces & Oils / Dairy & Chilled / Frozen / Drinks / Household & Other
catch-all), each with a keyword list and a `walkOrder` (the real render
sort order, matching a typical shop walk — Frozen deliberately walks
LAST despite being checked EARLY, see below).

`logic._classifyAisle(name)` — lowercase substring match against each
group's keywords, first hit wins, unmatched names fall into the honest
"Household & Other" catch-all rather than crashing or being dropped.

`ui._showShoppingList`'s "Need to buy" section (the one used while
actually shopping — "Already have enough"/"Already bought" are
deliberately left untouched, not the section causing the scrolling
complaint) now sorts by aisle walkOrder first, ingredient name second,
and renders a "📍 <Aisle>" subheading whenever the aisle changes.

A REAL BUG FOUND AND FIXED DURING VERIFICATION (rule 4 — one failed
check, get real evidence, don't ship blind)
------------------------------------------------------------
A first pass classified "Sichuan Peppercorn" as Fruit & Veg (a bare
"pepper" keyword matched inside "peppercorn") and "Vanilla Ice Cream" as
Spices (a bare "vanilla" keyword matched before Frozen's own "ice cream"
phrase was ever checked) — the exact keyword-substring collision class
CLAUDE.md's own landmines section already names for taxonomy keywords,
now proven to apply here too. Fixed with 2 real changes: (1) compound/
specific phrases ("bell pepper", "black pepper", "peppercorn") listed
ahead of the bare "pepper"/"cream" words wherever a real collision
exists; (2) match PRIORITY (array position, checked top-to-bottom) and
render WALK ORDER (`walkOrder` field) were deliberately split apart —
Frozen is checked early (right after Fruit & Veg) so "ice cream" wins
over Spices'/Dairy's bare keywords, but still RENDERS last (walkOrder 6)
matching a real shopping trip (frozen bought last so it doesn't thaw).
