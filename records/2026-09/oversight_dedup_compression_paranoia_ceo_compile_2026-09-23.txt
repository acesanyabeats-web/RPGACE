/debate — collapse-in-place vs. physical domain-split
===========================================================================
Side A (collapse-in-place): every fact still fits in CLAUDE.md, just
shrunk to durable one-liners + pointers into CLAUDE_archive.md/
patch_notes.html. Zero new drift surface — rule 16's own hard lesson this
same session ("a doc can be made false by a change made somewhere else
entirely") argues against multiplying files without a structural reason.

Side B (domain-split into separate state files): Alex explicitly asked to
"split info as much as possible to make individual page as clear and
concise and accurate as possible." CLAUDE.md's own precedent proves this
CAN work without becoming pure liability — interconnection_map.md/
system_flow_map.md/ai_tooling_and_rules_map.md are already split off, and
are described as read ON DEMAND ("before any nontrivial task"), never
auto-loaded, unlike CLAUDE.md. A per-domain split genuinely could shrink
the ALWAYS-loaded baseline itself, a different and larger saving than
collapsing alone produces.

/free-for-all-debate — individual real problems, not just the two sides:
  1. interconnection_map.md/system_flow_map.md's rule-violations are a
     small, independent, cheap fix regardless of the split-vs-collapse
     decision.
  2. Worked Precedents 1-6 (19,695 chars) are closed/stable and duplicate
     nothing live — a clean archival candidate under any scenario.
  3. patch_notes.html (1.3MB/423 cards) is NOT auto-loaded every session
     (a materially different cost profile from CLAUDE.md) — lower
     priority, flagged not ratified this pass (see Deferred, below).
  4. Prose discipline alone ("collapse when resolved") has already failed
     at this scale at least once (pre-July-31) and arguably worse this
     time — a prose reminder is not itself a fix, same lesson as this
     session's own SELF_KNOWLEDGE staleness work.
