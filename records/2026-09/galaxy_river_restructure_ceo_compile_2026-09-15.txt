=== REAL EVIDENCE — DIMENSIONS, INFRA VS. INTER ===

13 total Dimension pages, 3 real kinds (`DIMENSION_KIND_META`,
graphify_river_group.py):

    candidate by definition: Decision Matrix, Dimensions Matrix, Loops

  infra (5) — "a resource attached to things": Decisions/Human-Gates,

  inter (5) — "a connection/flow between things": Skills, Alex's
    Decision Path, Orchestrator↔OpenMontage, Oversight Sync, Local
    Analysis Pipeline

REAL FINDING, load-bearing, not previously surfaced in this session's
earlier /interrogation: `galaxy_map_hub.html`'s own live sidebar text
states the CURRENT, deliberate design reason Dimensions are not
rivers: "A Dimension is a real cross-cutting facet — the same modules
and functions, seen through one lens. Deliberately multi-membership —
which is exactly why a Dimension is never a numbered River (a River is
a strict one-module-one-home partition)." This is a real, stated
architectural decision, not an oversight — "fold Dimensions-Inter into
the river system" runs directly against it, and the compile step's
job is to surface that conflict plainly rather than build past it.

Two genuinely different ways to honor Alex's ask, both real,
structurally very different in cost/risk:

  (a) LITERAL — Inter dimensions become real numbered rivers (River
      XVIII+), added to RIVER_MODULES/MODULE_RIVER exactly like Rivers
      1-12. Real problem: RIVER_MODULES is currently a strict
      one-module-one-river partition (MODULE_RIVER is a plain dict,
      one river per module key) — a genuinely multi-membership Inter
      dimension (e.g. Skills touches dozens of modules across many
      existing rivers) cannot become a River without either (i)
      breaking that one-river-per-module invariant everywhere it's
      relied on (LEVEL3_MODULES, per-module coloring, the Current(L3)
      river-section grouping, `compute_intra_river_flow`'s own
      river-scoped edge computation), or (ii) reassigning modules away
      from their real existing river, which would be actively
      dishonest about where those modules really live.

  (b) SYMBOLIC/PRESENTATIONAL — Inter dimensions get River-style
      numbering/branding in navigation (a real precedent already
      exists: rivers 13-17 carry River numbers despite having zero
      real code-partition membership — they were always role labels,
      not strict partitions) without altering the underlying
      multi-membership semantics or touching `RIVER_MODULES`/
      `MODULE_RIVER`'s real one-river-per-module invariant. Real,
      lower-risk, and has a genuine existing precedent in this exact
      codebase to build from.
