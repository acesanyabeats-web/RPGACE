===========================================================================
What CLAUDE.md's own rule actually requires, re-read before starting
===========================================================================
Both docs are explicitly defined (CLAUDE.md's "Context/logging efficiency
rules") as "structural reference only, never a changelog... present
tense... If a paragraph is narrating a sequence of events rather than
describing current architecture, it belongs in patch_notes.html instead."
The key distinction applied throughout: a dated SNAPSHOT fact ("Live
count re-queried Aug 4: X rows") is CLAUDE.md's own sanctioned convention
and was left alone; a dated NARRATIVE phrase ("X shipped, then Y was
found wrong, then Z fixed it," or a bare "(built July NN)"/"NEW July NN"
tag announcing an event) is the actual violation and was rewritten to a
present-tense structural statement, with the historical detail either
dropped (if already told in full in patch_notes.html per rule 8) or
compressed+archived verbatim (if it was the ONLY copy of real information,
matching this session's own DD-1 archive-before-delete precedent).
