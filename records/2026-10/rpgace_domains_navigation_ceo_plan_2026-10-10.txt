== 7. LATE ADDITION — Deepstash-style Encyclopedia (Alex, same turn, verbatim) ==
"id like encyclopedia to make the articles like a post or series from deepstash to make it more productive.
/paranoia and /CEO and /drift"

Deepstash shape: an article becomes a short series of "idea" cards (one idea = a title + 2-4 sentences,
read one at a time, swipe/next), each idea can be saved on its own, and related posts group into a series.

Evidence it fits what exists (no new content needed):
- Each CI report already splits into list-shaped parts: summary, production_techniques, what_to_steal,
  what_to_avoid, content_strategy_insights, quests. Each list item = one idea card.
- encyclopedia_insights (106 rows) is already "one insight per row, linked to its source article,
  with macro_category". That IS a Deepstash idea table. Reuse it (rule 8), don't add a second one.
- Tags on every encyclopedia_entry + macro_category = the grouping for a series.

C1 amended: articles render as an idea-card post by default (progress dots "3 / 9", Next/Back, read-time
label, "Save idea" per card, "Full article" one tap away), and the Encyclopedia home shows Series
(articles grouped by tag/category) instead of one long list.

/paranoia:
- Risk: cards hide context. Your earlier ask was "access whole articles". Fix: the full article always sits one
  tap away, and every card links back to it.
- Risk: card text written by the AI as a list can read as fragments. The backfill only splits what is
  already split; no rewording pass (rewording costs tokens and can drift from the source).
- Risk: "Save idea" needs storage. Use a status value on encyclopedia_insights (e.g. 'saved'). Before
  writing the new value, query pg_constraint first (Oct 10 lesson). Additive only.
- Risk: duplicate card UI. Bookworm chapters and Jargon already have card lists; build one shared
  idea-card renderer and let Jargon reuse it later, not a third copy.
- Swipe on phone: reuse button Next/Back (thumb-sized), not a new touch-gesture handler — the app has
  an unresolved swipe-freeze history (leftNav).

/drift: on track. One real ambiguity: "post or series" — post = one article split into ideas;
series = several articles grouped. The plan does both; Alex confirms (fork F4).

F4 Deepstash: both (each article = a post of idea cards, articles grouped into series by topic)
   (recommended), or only the per-article card post?
