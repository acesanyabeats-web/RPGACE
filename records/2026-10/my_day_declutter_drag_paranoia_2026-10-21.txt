FREE-FOR-ALL DEBATE (5 competitors, 2 problems each)
1 Calendar purist: (a) the day timeline IS the home - Now line, blocks, gaps labelled "45 min free +"; (b) Schedule calendar
  uses the same component so drag works the same in both places.
2 Declutterer (Alex's swipe list): (a) home shows only Now / Next / today's timeline; everything else (Plan week, Review,
  trackers, cut-backs, templates, alerts, manage routines, realm quests) moves into a shortcut list opened by a swipe;
  (b) the realm strip + quest generator + "Taken in/Knows" belong to XP/Knowledge pages, not My Day.
3 Phone ergonomics (paranoia): (a) drag fights page scroll -> pick up with a long-press (~300ms) + haptic tick, then drag;
  quick swipe still scrolls; (b) SWIPE CONFLICTS: iPhone's left-edge swipe = Back, and leftNav already has its own swipe
  gesture (the landmine area). A swipe list must start away from the screen edge, or open from a visible handle/button too.
4 Speed: (a) one component + CSS classes, render only today's blocks first, sheets built on demand -> target under 0.5s;
  (b) optimistic drag (block moves instantly, saves in background, snaps back + toast if the save fails).
5 Skeptic / why not: (a) MD-1..8 logic works and is tested - a UI rebuild must not touch it; (b) page-quests is shared with
  other modules - moving their cards touches XP/Knowledge too, so do it as a separate, small step.

MORE DRAG IDEAS ("think of more")

- Tray of things to place (due chores, routines due today, realm items, quest ideas, a recipe to cook with its real
  duration) -> drag onto an empty slot to schedule it.
- Drag tasks up/down inside a block, or into another block.
- Tap an empty gap -> new block filling that gap; drag on empty space -> draw a block of that length.
- Swipe a block right = done, left = move to tomorrow (with Undo).
- Drop a block on a "Tomorrow" / "Unplanned" dock at the bottom to bump it.
- Pinch to zoom the timeline (15-min detail <-> whole day).
- Shifts are locked: dragging into one stops at its edge and says why.
- Too-long item dropped into a short gap -> the existing "shorten X?" push rule.
- Haptic tick on each 5-min snap; Undo bar after every drag (exists).
