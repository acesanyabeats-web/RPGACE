THE ONE FOUNDATION ITEM ("then just ask"):
  X1 "Ask my data" — Oracle can request a read-only lookup on allowlisted tables via a single-line trailer
  (e.g. ORACLE_QUERY: table | filter), the client runs RPGACE.sb.select (anon read already allowed), and Oracle
  answers from the real rows. Many A/B ideas become ordinary questions instead of separate features.
  Constraints: single-line trailer (the numbered-list landmine), row cap + column cap (token rule 11), allowlist
  excludes secrets/meta tables, two Oracle calls per lookup (cost named, not hidden).
