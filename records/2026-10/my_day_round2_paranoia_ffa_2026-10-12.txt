2. FREE-FOR-ALL DEBATE (4 problems, competitors each took 2)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
P1 Shift auto-import   P2 Multi-week planning of weeklies   P3 Monthly days-since zones   P4 Healthy-habit coverage

Competitor A "Fix the pipe first" (P1, P2)
- P1: before any new source, every import path must write rpgace_shifts (through /api/data-write) and shiftSync
  must MERGE not overwrite. Otherwise any new automation repeats the same silent wipe. Fix the BST date bug and
  stop hardcoding the role. Retire DEFAULT_SHIFTS.
- P2: Plan my day already exists for "today"; extend the same slot engine to a week view rather than inventing a
  second planner (rule 8).

Competitor B "Zero-touch import" (P1, P3)
- P1: the PC script can never be "auto" (headed browser, manual paste, laptop on). Options, best first:
  (a) If the Fourth/HotSchedules phone app syncs shifts into his phone calendar (Google/Apple), RPGACE reads that
      calendar's private iCal link on the cloud runner (GitHub Actions, same pattern as the video queue) -> fully
      automatic, no Fourth password stored anywhere. Needs: does his Fourth app sync to a calendar?
  (b) Screenshot route: share the rota screenshot to RPGACE (S1 share target already exists) -> Screenshot inbox
      gains a "Shifts" home -> Oracle vision extracts shifts -> confirm -> rpgace_shifts. Semi-automatic, works
      from the phone, no credentials.
  (c) Cloud Playwright login to Fourth with his username/password as GitHub secrets. Most automatic but: storing
      a work password (Tier 3 credential), possible MFA/captcha, Fourth's terms, breaks when Fourth changes its
      page. Last resort.
  (d) Keep the PC script but make it POST to Supabase instead of the console-paste step.
- A vs B on P1: A says "no new source until the pipe holds"; B says "the pipe fix alone doesn't make it automatic".
  MERGED: both — pipe fix is step 1 (cheap, unblocks everything), then one automatic source chosen by Alex.
- P3: "monthly" is really "every N days with a tolerance". Store a target interval per routine (e.g. 30 days) and
  colour by days since last done: green < ~70% of interval, yellow until due, red overdue. One mechanism covers
  monthly, fortnightly, quarterly (deep clean oven, haircut, change toothbrush, back up beats, defrost freezer).

Competitor C "Plan the week, not the day" (P2, P4)
- P2: a "This week / Next week / Week after" planner: each weekly or N-per-week routine shows a row of 7 day-chips
  per week; Alex taps the days it happens that week (pre-filled from its usual days), shifts shown on each day so
  he can avoid them. Planned days write to the schedule (rpgace_agendas) with the routine id, so Today, streaks
  and Plan my day read one source.
  Disagreement with A: A wants to reuse the today-slot engine for times; C says weekly planning is about DAYS,
  times can stay optional per day. MERGED: day-picking first, optional "suggest times" uses A's engine.
- P4: health coverage should be a checklist of domains (sleep, movement, food, water, mind, social, learning,
  money, home, creative) with the ideas menu showing gaps ("nothing for sleep yet") — not a giant fixed list.

Competitor D "Habits that stick" (P3, P4)
- P3: red zone must DO something, not just colour: a red routine appears on Today and in Plan my day suggestions
  automatically; yellow shows on the week planner as "schedule soon".
- P4: research-backed habit design: tiny start version ("2 pages" vs "20 pages"), habit stacking ("after coffee,
  read"), cue + reward, minimum vs target ("floor/goal"), never miss twice, weekly review. Psychology reading
  connects here: a "Psychology" area with reading + "apply one idea this week" + journal reflection.
  Disagreement with C: C's domain checklist could become nagging; D wants Alex to choose which domains matter.
  Left as a question for Alex.

Survived as weak / dropped
- Auto-generating a full routine set from AI: dropped (Alex explicitly wants to pick).
- Streak punishment / XP loss for missing: dropped unless Alex asks (shame mechanics hurt adherence).
- Wearable/sleep tracker integration: parked — needs to know what devices he has.
