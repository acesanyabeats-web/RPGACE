      so the phone only plays what RPGACE picked.
 PA-3 Phone side: a step-by-step MacroDroid guide + one small feed URL (next wake alarm: time, sound URL, label) the phone
      reads; it rings that sound at that time, repeating until stopped; "I'm up" in RPGACE also marks it done. Exact MacroDroid
      steps verified against its current docs at build time (free tier: 5 macros - this needs 1-2).
 PA-4 Honest status: My Day shows whether a phone is connected (last time the phone read the feed) and warns when nothing can
      ring; notifications say plainly when no phone is registered (push_subscriptions = 0 today).
 Council of 5: risk = Android battery saving can delay MacroDroid (guide includes "don't optimise" setting; feed read near
 alarm time); cost = free (MacroDroid free tier, Supabase storage tiny); UX = your sounds, random, no tap per alarm; why-not =
 one more app on the phone and a one-time setup you must do yourself; a public sound bucket means anyone with the link can play
 the files (they are alarm sounds, not private).
