PARANOIA STEP 1 — RESTRUCTURE / AINTERGRATION (short)
- No third-party tool needed: a pomodoro is a timer loop; the Doing Now session + Cook Mode timer bar already give
  the parts (start/stop, minutes saved, XP). Adopt the PRINCIPLE (focus/break cycles), build on doingNow.
- Restructure win: one timeline (shifts + schedule + blocks + travel buffers) replaces 2 hand-rolled busy lists.

PARANOIA STEP 3 — FREE-FOR-ALL DEBATE (5 competitors, each picks 2 problems)
1. Timeboxer — (a) durations as snap presets (25/50/90 + custom), 15-min grid; (b) drag-to-resize disallowed past
   the next busy edge, so overlap is impossible by construction rather than warned about.
2. Pomodoro purist — (a) a block = N focus rounds (25 on / 5 off, long break after 4), so "90 min Producer" is 3
   rounds + breaks; (b) each finished round ticks progress on the block's items and logs focus minutes (XP once).
3. Shift-worker realist — (a) protocols must fit a chef's rota: after a late shift no deep-work block before 10am;
   energy-aware placement (deep work when fresh, admin when tired); (b) breaks/buffers between blocks auto-added
   (travel, get-ready already exist) so a day can't be packed wall-to-wall.
4. Minimalist — (a) timers are friction; most blocks just need a tick; protocol must be optional per block;
   (b) warns against 5 protocols nobody uses — pick 2-3 max.
5. Dedup engineer — (a) one busy-timeline function (logic.busy(date)) used by Plan week, Plan tomorrow, templates,
   cook/shopping blocks; (b) one timer engine shared by Cook Mode, Doing Now and focus rounds, not a 3rd copy.
VERDICT (Council of 5): overlap must be prevented at placement (not just warned); one busy timeline + one timer
engine are non-negotiable (rule 8); protocols optional per block, small menu. Real forks for Alex below.

PARANOIA STEP 5 — FACT-CHECK (Council of 5 + simplify)
- Phone alerts mid-block: the only push today is the hourly myday-push cron (pg_cron '2 * * * *'). A round ending
  while the app is in the background needs its own due-alert queue + a once-a-minute job that only sends when a row
  is due (cheap, free tier). Alerts can be up to ~1 minute late; in-app alerts are exact. Named, not hidden.
- Drag on a phone is fiddly: big handles, 5-minute snapping, and the drag stops at a neighbour/bookend so it can't
  overlap mid-drag. Tap a block -> exact start/length fields as a fallback.
- Week templates: reuse day_templates (0 rows, already a "saved layout" table) with a week kind rather than a new
  table, if its columns fit; otherwise one small additive table. Decided at that step's brief.
- Dedup (rule 8): ONE timeline engine (busy + push + bookends + buffers) used by every planner; ONE timer engine shared
  with Cook Mode's timer bar and Doing Now. The two existing busy calculators (clashes, suggestSlots) move onto it.
- Why NOT: a lot of planning machinery for a ~1 tick/day user. Answered by keeping Do one tap and templates doing the
  weekly work; every method is optional per block.
