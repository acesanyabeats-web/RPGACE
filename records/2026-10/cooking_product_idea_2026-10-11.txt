== The idea (verbatim) ==
"the cooking module, we can create that as a separate app for batch cooking and making it a more accessible service
where people can take into account what they actually have and make recipes out of it ... Give them a few spices that
they just invest in if they like a particular cuisine and then they can reuse them and then just slowly build out the
pantry ... especially if it like counts macros and all that and it could really help with like gym and stuff so
scheduling as well ... you do prep like one day when you buy the groceries you hook one recipe and then if they plan
another recipe for the week They've already got that prep done so we can do different prep phases cook phases if you
want to cook two meals at the same time ... so many different modes and we can genuinely sell this ... I think we
should do this as a separate project"
