PLAN ITEMS (each briefed before it is built, rule 18)
SH-0  Selling home: /selling page (sellingHub) with two tracks — 🥁 Beats for sale (List → Promote → Sell → Paid) and

SH-2  Pitches: make a pitch (what you offer: pick beats from My beats, a pack, exclusive, collab…; message; ask price);

SH-3  Follow up + Deal + Paid: stage buttons on each send; follow-up Due rows (no reply after N days, default 5);
      Deal step records terms + amount with Contract Red Flags + Negotiation Rehearsal at hand; agreed-but-unpaid shows as
      money owed; Paid logs the sale (chronicles_finance, linked beat) → Hustler XP; a collab deal also counts collab_done.
SH-4  Beats for sale track: default licence prices (your standard tiers prefilled on every beat); List = released beats
      with / without a selling link; Promote = A&R Critic on that beat + its posts; Sell = Log a sale (BT-8 path).
SH-5  Money: monthly income target (progress on Selling home + My Day, Due when behind), earnings per beat + best
      sellers. Extra scenarios to pick from at the SH-5 brief: money owed, income by type (lease / exclusive /
      placement / collab / royalties typed in), profit (beat costs like samples/plugins vs income), pitch → deal rate
      per pitch, best artist contacts. Hustler milestone proof: 'zip_or_sale' → sale.
SH-6  Move the Business tools into the steps (Outreach at Pitch, Contract + Negotiation at Deal, A&R Critic on a beat;
      Playlist Psychologist → Beats' Idea step); Captions panel keeps captions only.
SH-7  Cross-links (beat ↔ its pitches and sales, artist ↔ People, Chronicles entries for deals/payments), menu order,
      full tests, phone check, docs, SELF_KNOWLEDGE, Galaxy regen, plan done.
