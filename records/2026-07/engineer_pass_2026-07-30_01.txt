  1 query, now 2 in parallel) - negligible cost, well within rule 11's
  design-constraint intent (this is not a premium-model call, it's a
  free REST read).

ITEM 4 - Auto-advance stage triggers (videoPipeline._showDetails save
handler + new _computeAutoAdvancedStatus):
  WHAT CHANGED: saving raw_path/edited_path/rendered_path/export_paths
  now computes the furthest stage the newly-saved data supports and
  writes it into `status` alongside the path fields, never moving
  status backward (max of current stage index and computed stage
  index).
  REAL JUDGMENT CALL MADE, NOT LEFT UNDECIDED (per the goal's own
  instruction): the FIRST populated export_paths entry advances status
  straight to 'exported', not all 4 targets. Reasoning documented
  inline in the code: F16's own Beatstars button only shows once a
  licence_type is set, meaning a non-sale video would NEVER get all 4
  exports and could never reach 'exported' if all 4 were required -
  this would make the terminal stage unreachable for most real videos.
  RESIDUAL RISK: a genuinely unusual workflow (e.g. someone deliberately
  wants to track "partially exported" as distinct from "fully exported"
  across all 4 platforms) is not served by this - flagged honestly, not
  hidden, and easy to revisit if it turns out to matter in practice.

ITEM 5 - Backfill popup (refCorpus._showBackfillPopup + a new toolbar
