A Dimension is a real cross-cutting facet — the same modules and functions, seen through one lens. Deliberately multi-membership: a module can sit in several at once, which is exactly why a Dimension is never a numbered River (a River is a strict one-module-one-home partition). Nested under whichever Level(s) it's genuinely relevant to, not a separate category.
