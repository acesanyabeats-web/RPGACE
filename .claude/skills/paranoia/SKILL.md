# /paranoia — maximum scrutiny for one proposed update

Alex, Aug 6, verbatim: *"use /restructure and /aintergration in tandem to see what is purely possible and what outcomes will come of it using /scope, /godmode and /commit-archaeologist with /omnitrix and /5thDimension without fable with opus 5, then /debate and /free-for-all-debate where needed to see what will work with my workflow and ideas logged and implemented with /interrogation to get my opinions into it, then council of 5 this outcome with /simplify and /scope /godmode and /commit-archaeologist to fact check all of it and give me a /Summary of all i can and cant do, what implications and obstacle come with it, and what improvements in code, intercommunication of agents ai buttons doms and functions are, and how output will be improved. use /Engineer to supervise all stages."* He named it /paranoia: *"it visualises how an update will shape and give honest pros and cons on goal set."*

## Steps
1. **What's possible**: Aintergration (worth adopting? for the app, the dev session, or both) and `/restructure` (would replacing existing infrastructure make code shorter, faster or the output better?), run on the same evidence.
2. **Reconcile with real state**: `/scope` + GODMODE (live code, git, Supabase, deploys), `commit-archaeologist` (why the current thing exists), Omnitrix (no Fable) and `/5thDimension` (built vs reported, Alex's goals).
3. **Test against his workflow**: `/debate`; add `/free-for-all-debate` when there are more than two real shapes. This catches "technically right, wrong for how Alex works".
4. **`/interrogation`**: real forks go to Alex as questions. A pass that never asks him anything has skipped this step.
5. **Fact-check** — an independent pass: Council of 5, `/simplify` (reuse, efficiency), `/scope` + GODMODE + `commit-archaeologist` re-verifying every claim. This is where mistakes are caught.
6. **`/Summary`**: what he can and can't do, implications and obstacles, improvements to code and to how agents/AI/buttons/DOM/functions communicate, and the change in output quality. A report, never a build; Tier 3 items still need his explicit go.
Throughout, `/Engineer` supervises: goal contract, evidence at every step, a report written from what actually happened, and a Truth Check before it's handed over.

If Steps 2 or 5 contradict an existing `perspective_reports` baseline, that's a regression: route it through `/perspective` Step 5 (error code, `error_log` row, purple if it was green).

## Guardrails
- Reserve it for one change Alex wants maximally scrutinised.
- Token cost still matters. If Alex is resource-constrained, say so and propose a right-sized version that reuses existing evidence (CEO Loop 1 allows `/drift` + plain scrutiny for small finding sets, with `/paranoia` weight on the MATERIAL ones — state the deviation).
- No manufactured balance: if the answer is clearly yes or clearly no, say it.
- A verdict isn't authorisation.
